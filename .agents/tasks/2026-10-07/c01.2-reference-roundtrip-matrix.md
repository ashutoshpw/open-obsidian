## Fixture requirements

Keep the reference fixture synthetic or scrubbed of personal data. Include a
UTF-8 BOM and CRLF Markdown note, nested and unknown frontmatter, Unicode and
space-containing paths, attachments with original names, unknown binary data,
`.obsidian` settings with an unknown key, and representative plugin/theme data.
Store the fixture's provenance, app version, per-file SHA-256 values, and any
expected workspace-state allowlist beside the fixture.

Do not mark C01.2 passing from C01.2-02 alone. C01.2 stays pending until the
reference provenance and reopen cases have CI evidence for every available OS.
